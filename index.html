Jenny's Blurbs

About me:

I'm a Staff Engineer in Developer Relations at Edge Impulse (now part of Qualcomm), based in Amsterdam. For over five years I've helped developers, researchers, and companies take machine learning from the cloud to the constraint: tiny sensors, microcontrollers, and the devices that actually touch the physical world.

Before Edge Impulse I spent three years at Arm working on Mbed OS and the Pelion IoT platform, and I studied Electrical & Computer Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin. I co-wrote AI at the Edge for O'Reilly, and these days I also build apps with AI.

Who I'd like to meet:

Developers, researchers, and teams taking ML from the cloud to the constraint. Conference and webinar organizers who need someone to make edge AI make sense. Anyone with a weird sensor and a good idea.
